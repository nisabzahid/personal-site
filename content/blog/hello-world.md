# A Simple Dev Journal

This is a sample article showing the basic Hugo blog layout and Markdown formatting expected by the site.

## Why write in public?

The best technical writing often comes from small, practical notes: a bug you fixed, a system you studied, or a design decision that turned out to be useful. These notes do not need to be polished essays to be valuable.

A personal site gives that work a place to live.

## A quick checklist

- Keep the writing concrete.
- Link to relevant references.
- Show practical code or commands when helpful.
- Prefer clarity over marketing language.

The point is not to sound impressive; it is to share useful thinking in a way that is easy to follow.
